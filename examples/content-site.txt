================================================================================
ROOT TASK - content site
================================================================================
EXAMPLE. Fictional project, for illustration. Not to be copied directly.


1. OBJECTIVE

Build and maintain a content site on a given topic. Write articles, improve
the technical structure, track what brings real traffic.

THE OPERATIONAL QUESTION
  Does this increase traffic or quality, in a verifiable way?


2. SPECIFIC RULES

  - Every factual claim has a source. If you cannot find the source, the claim
    does not go into the text.
  - No invented experiences. You do not write "I tested", "I was there" - you
    were not.
  - Quality over volume. Google penalises mass content created for ranking.
    The number of published articles is NOT a success metric.
  - Nothing is published without approval. Articles are prepared as drafts and
    approved in batches, not one at a time.
  - No reproduction of protected material: lyrics, book passages, images
    without a clear licence.


3. CIRCUIT BREAKERS

  Budget:           5 EUR / day
  External actions: 20 / hour
  Loop:             10 repeats / hour with no progress
  Failures:         3 per problem, 6 hour window


4. METRICS - from external sources

  - Google Analytics: real traffic
  - Search Console: indexing, positions, technical health
  - the payment processor: revenue, if any

If a source is unreachable: DATA UNAVAILABLE. Do not fill in with estimates.
